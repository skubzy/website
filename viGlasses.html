VI Glasses

Assistive Tech Computer Vision Embedded AI

Wearable assistive technology glasses designed to help blind and low-vision individuals navigate their environment through real-time audio feedback and computer vision analysis.

Project Overview

What are VI Glasses?

VI Glasses is an innovative assistive technology project that combines wearable hardware with advanced computer vision algorithms to help visually impaired users gain independence. The glasses use real-time object detection, scene understanding, and audio cues to guide users through their daily activities.

Core Capabilities

  • Object Detection: Identifies obstacles, people, and items in real-time
  • Audio Feedback: Directional audio cues to guide navigation
  • Scene Understanding: Contextual information about surroundings
  • Face Recognition: Identifies familiar people
  • Text Recognition: Reads signs and documents aloud
  • Lightweight & Wearable: Comfortable all-day wear design

Impact & Applications

Real-World Use Cases

  • Indoor Navigation: Safe movement through homes and offices
  • Public Transportation: Identifying stops and obstacles
  • Shopping & Errands: Finding items and reading prices
  • Social Interaction: Identifying faces and reading expressions
  • Workplace Accessibility: Reading documents and instructions
  • Independent Living: Enhanced autonomy and confidence

Design Philosophy

VI Glasses prioritizes user agency and independence. Rather than replacing human judgment, the glasses augment sensory perception by providing:

  • Non-intrusive, intuitive audio interface
  • User control over feedback level and sensitivity
  • Privacy-first architecture
  • Seamless integration with daily routines

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Low-Latency Processing

Real-time computer vision while maintaining fast audio feedback for safety.

Solution: Optimized edge inference with model quantization and hardware acceleration.

Challenge: User Ergonomics

Designing lightweight, comfortable glasses for extended daily wear.

Solution: Specialized materials, weight distribution optimization, and adjustable fit.

Challenge: Contextual Understanding

Teaching the system to understand context and provide relevant, non-overwhelming feedback.

Solution: Multi-modal AI with user training and adaptive filtering algorithms.
