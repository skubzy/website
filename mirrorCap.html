Project Overview

What is Mirror Cap?

Mirror Cap is an innovative wearable assistive device that augments situational awareness through multi-sensor feedback. The glasses combine ultrasonic proximity detection, light sensors (LDR), haptic feedback, and audio alerts to provide real-time environmental information.

Core Features

  • Ultrasonic Sensors: Real-time obstacle detection and distance measurement
  • Light Dependent Resistor (LDR): Ambient light sensing for adaptive feedback
  • Haptic Feedback: Vibration alerts for non-visual cues
  • Audio Buzzer: Multi-tone alert system for warnings
  • Lightweight, wearable design for all-day use
  • Low power consumption for extended battery life

Sensor Integration

Ultrasonic Detection

The ultrasonic sensors measure distance to obstacles in real-time, providing continuous proximity awareness. Distance thresholds trigger haptic or audio alerts based on urgency.

Light Adaptation

The LDR sensor adjusts feedback intensity based on ambient lighting conditions, ensuring audible and haptic signals are always appropriate for the environment.

Multi-Modal Feedback

  • Haptic vibration for subtle, discreet alerts
  • Audio tones with varying frequencies for urgency levels
  • Adaptive feedback intensity based on environment
  • User-customizable alert thresholds

Design Challenges

Challenge: Sensor Interference

Ultrasonic sensors can interfere with each other and be affected by environmental conditions.

Solution: Staggered sensor activation, filtering algorithms, and environmental calibration.

Challenge: Power Efficiency

Continuous sensor polling drains battery quickly in wearable devices.

Solution: Optimized polling intervals, sleep modes, and selective sensor activation.

Challenge: User Comfort

Designing alerts that are effective without being intrusive or annoying.

Solution: Programmable thresholds, adaptive feedback, and user-centered design testing.
